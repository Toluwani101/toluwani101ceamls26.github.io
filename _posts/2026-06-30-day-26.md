what_i_learned: |
  Today, we attended the AWS Summit in Washington, D.C., which was a valuable and exciting professional experience. We traveled there by bus in the morning, and the trip took about an hour and thirty minutes. After arriving and picking up my conference badge, I had the chance to take pictures around the venue, including with the AWS logo, which made the experience feel even more memorable. I also listened to the keynote speech, where I learned more about AWS, cloud technology, and how different companies are using cloud services to solve real-world problems. One of the most valuable parts of the day was the opportunity to network with industry professionals from Amazon and several companies partnered with AWS. I spoke with representatives from companies such as Datadog, AMD, Commvault, and CrowdStrike, and I learned more about their work and how their technologies connect with AWS services. I also connected with some of the professionals I met on LinkedIn, which helped me begin building stronger professional relationships in the technology industry. Toward the end of the day, one professional asked me a question that really stayed with me: “What do you want to do with AI?” Although I was able to introduce myself and explain my interests, that question made me realize that I should continue thinking deeply about my long-term goals in AI and machine learning. Overall, attending the AWS Summit helped me learn more about cloud computing, industry applications of AI, and the importance of building meaningful professional connections.
