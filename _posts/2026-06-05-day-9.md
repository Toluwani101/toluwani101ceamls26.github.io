  Today, I learned a great deal about Computer Vision and the technologies that power object detection systems. During the session, I learned more about YOLO (You Only Look Once) and how to import and use it in Python. We practiced applying YOLO to identify and detect objects in different images and videos, which helped me better understand how computer vision models can analyze visual data in real time. I also learned more about OpenCV and how it can be used to manipulate images and videos, including tasks such as resizing and preprocessing visual data before it is analyzed by a model. The quizzes were both engaging and helpful because they clarified several concepts and allowed me to test what I had learned throughout the session. It was a good way to identify areas I understood well and topics that needed further review. Learning about the different research topics and approaches that other teams are working on was both interesting and inspiring, and it gave me a broader perspective on the variety of problems that can be addressed using technology and research.
