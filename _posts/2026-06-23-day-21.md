  Today was a combination of administrative tasks, team-building activities, and research work. We started the day with a walkthrough on how to complete our timesheets so that we can receive our pay throughout the program. I ran into a few issues during the process because I did not yet have a Morgan email address, but it was still helpful to learn how the system works and what steps need to be completed moving forward. After that, we were divided into two groups and played a game called Taboo, where one person had to describe a word without using a list of restricted words while their teammates tried to guess it. I was on Group A, and although my team did not win, it was a fun activity that encouraged communication, quick thinking, and teamwork. It was also a great opportunity to interact with other participants outside of our usual research activities. During the second half of the day, we returned to our research labs, where I spent time training different YOLO models, including YOLOv8-P2, YOLOv8, YOLOv11, and YOLOv26. Working with multiple models helped me better understand how different architectures can affect object detection performance and accuracy. While the models were training, I also continued working on my literature review introduction, focusing specifically on identifying the research gap and clearly defining the research objectives of our project. Overall, today helped me strengthen both my technical skills and my understanding of how to communicate the purpose and significance of our research.
