what_i_learned: |
  Today, we spent the first half of the day in the lab working on different parts of our project. One of our YOLO models finished training, so we documented the results and reviewed its performance. After that, my team and I spent most of the lab session trying to find a solution for scaling the QR code that works with our robotic arm. I tried my best to explain what I understood from our faculty mentor’s discussion the day before, especially the idea of connecting image measurements to real-world robot movement. As a team, we worked on applying that information to the QR code scaling problem, and we were able to make some progress. During the second half of the day, we had SAIRI Connect, where all the teams gathered together for group activities. We first played a name game where everyone switched names and had to remember their new identities, which was a fun way to interact with other participants. Afterward, we watched an AI-related movie called *Mercy*, starring Chris Pratt, one of my favorite actors. The movie was about a detective who had to defend himself in an AI courtroom after being accused of his wife’s murder, with much of the evidence pointing toward him. Reflecting on the movie, it made me think more deeply about the role of AI in society. Although AI can be powerful and useful, the movie reminded me that not everything should be fully controlled by AI. Some decisions still require human judgment, empathy, and critical thinking to ensure fairness and accountability.
