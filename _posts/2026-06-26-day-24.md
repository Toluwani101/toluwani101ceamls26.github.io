  Today, we spent most of the day working on our research presentation and making final improvements before the Mid-Summer Presentation on Monday. We reviewed the feedback from our faculty mentor and graduate mentor and made several corrections to improve the clarity, structure, and overall flow of our slides. We also practiced our presentation multiple times to make sure everyone understood their role and knew what they were responsible for saying. In addition, we tested our timing to ensure that our group stayed within the required presentation time frame while still explaining the important parts of our project clearly. We also spent time working on our demo video. After a few retakes, we were able to record a strong version that showed us testing our YOLO model with a webcam and LEGO pieces. This demo helped show the practical progress we have made so far and gave us a visual way to explain how our object detection model works. Our faculty mentor also brought in the robotic arm today and gave us a brief explanation of how it works, which was exciting because it connected our YOLO model training to the larger goal of robotic LEGO assembly. We were also informed about an AWS Conference in Washington, D.C., that we will be attending next Tuesday, and I am really interested in learning more from that experience. Finally, we had a mock presentation session where we presented to other groups in preparation for the main Mid-Summer Presentation. Overall, today helped us refine our work, improve our confidence, and prepare to present our research more professionally.
